How it works and limitations

Browser-only DSP: your microphone is connected to an AnalyserNode. The app calculates RMS amplitude, dominant frequency, spectral centroid, and low/mid/high energy bands locally. Those features produce an interpretable sound profile such as speech-like, music-like, bass-heavy, or bright/noisy.

This is not a trained YAMNet model and cannot identify every AudioSet class. It is a lightweight signal-analysis baseline: microphone gain affects dBFS, overlapping sounds can confuse the heuristic, and no audio is uploaded or stored.
